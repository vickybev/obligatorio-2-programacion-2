## Prompt

Original hand-painted anime fantasy game background, 4:3 landscape. Cozy gothic witch's confectionery kitchen at night, rich painterly cel-animation style, delicate ink linework, deep plum and navy with teal jade and dusty rose accents, warm amber lantern light. Ornate shelves of tiny potion bottles and jars of sweets along the outer left and right edges, lush trailing plants, floral decorations, a moonlit window across the upper rear wall. A wide uncluttered dark purple working space fills central 80 percent of frame, subtle low contrast backdrop suitable for tiny falling candy icons to be readable. Detailed decorations confined to outermost edges and top 15 percent. A narrow wooden counter only along bottom edge. No characters, no creatures, no loose falling objects in center, no text or UI, no visible basket. Dreamy original anime storybook atmosphere, polished full bleed illustration.
