## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original hand painted anime fantasy game background, landscape 4:3 composition. A lush enchanted nocturnal greenhouse garden, gothic cozy mood, deep indigo and plum shadows, jade vegetation, abundant climbing vines, pink flowering branches and subtle warm lanterns, moonlit blue atmospheric depth. Delicate ink outlines and rich painterly cel-animation background craftsmanship, organic detail like a beautifully drawn animated film environment. Composition for an arcade: detailed vegetation frames the outer edges and corners, middle 80 percent is a spacious relatively dark flat mossy clearing with minimal low contrast texture so tiny moving yellow and purple creatures remain clearly readable. No characters, no creatures, no bag, no buildings in middle, no text, no UI, no borders. A small distant greenhouse arch on top edge, dreamy luminous moon through leaves at top right. Full bleed finished illustration, no watermarks.
